Privacy Policy

Effective date: 25 October 2026

Butterfly is designed to help users manage their social media presence from one place. This policy explains how Butterfly may collect, use, store and disclose information when users use the service or connect third-party accounts.

1. Information We Handle

Depending on the features you use, Butterfly may handle account details such as your username and email address, content you create, media you upload, platform selections, and information returned by social platforms you choose to connect.

When you connect a third-party account, Butterfly may receive account identifiers, profile information and authorization credentials permitted by that platform's authorization process and the permissions you grant.

2. How We Use Information

Information is used to operate and maintain Butterfly, authenticate users, connect supported social accounts, carry out publishing actions you request, troubleshoot problems, and protect the service against abuse.

3. Third-Party Platforms

When you connect a social media platform, your use of that platform remains subject to its own terms and privacy policy. Data obtained through a platform is handled according to the permissions granted, applicable platform rules, and this policy.

Butterfly does not claim ownership of the content you create. You remain responsible for having the rights and permissions needed to publish content you submit.

4. Storage and Security

Butterfly uses technical and organizational measures intended to protect information. No method of storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Before this policy is finalized, Butterfly's actual practices for storing account data, protecting access tokens, retention, backups and access controls must be documented here accurately.

5. Sharing and Disclosure

Information may be processed by service providers that help operate Butterfly, or disclosed where required by law or necessary to protect the service and its users. Information may also be sent to a social platform when you request an action that requires it, such as publishing a post.

Butterfly's service-provider arrangements and any other data disclosures should be listed here before public launch.

6. Retention and Deletion

Information should be retained only for as long as needed for the purposes described in this policy, subject to applicable legal, security and operational requirements.

Contact Butterfly to ask about accessing or deleting your personal information, disconnecting a social account, or withdrawing authorization. The final policy must specify the available request process, applicable exceptions, and how connected-platform data and stored credentials are removed.

7. Your Choices

You can choose whether to connect a third-party account and which available publishing actions to initiate. You may also revoke Butterfly's access through the relevant third-party platform, where that option is available.

8. Children's Privacy

Butterfly is not intended for children who are not permitted to use the service under applicable law or the terms of the relevant platforms.

9. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated as Butterfly evolves. The effective date above should be revised when material changes are made.

10. Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact: Butterfly@gmail.com.
