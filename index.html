Why Butterfly

Technology should bring things together, not pull us apart.

The digital world offers incredible ways to create, communicate, and build communities. But the tools we use can leave our digital lives fragmented.

Butterfly starts with social media management and aims to make that experience simpler, more coherent, and more user-controlled.

Built around people Tools should serve the people using them.
Clarity over complexity A more straightforward way to manage digital workflows.
Control stays with you Make informed choices about your accounts and content.
